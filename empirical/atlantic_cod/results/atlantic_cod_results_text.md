We used the Atlantic cod supergene dataset as a multi-population test of whether recombination-partitioned chromosomal backgrounds are associated with persistent local genealogy structure. Arrangement-derived quartet predictions were frozen independently of local trees, and a baseline population-history prediction was frozen from collinear sequence. We then evaluated published 250-kb SNAPP population-tree summaries using a window-level contrast D(w)=fA−fB, where fA is the fraction of informative quartets matching the arrangement partition and fB is the fraction matching the baseline population-history partition.

Local genealogies within LG01 and LG02 shifted strongly toward the independently defined arrangement partition within the inversion intervals. LG01 had mean inside D=1.000, ΔD=1.233, exact circular-shift p=0.009, and BH-adjusted p=0.022. LG02 had ΔD=0.892, exact circular-shift p=0.011, and BH-adjusted p=0.022. These effects were robust to contiguous-block and physical-coordinate-aware nulls and to leave-one-population-out summaries.

LG12 also showed a positive arrangement-associated shift (ΔD=1.043, raw circular-shift p=0.038, BH-adjusted p=0.051), but we retain the predeclared caveats that LG12 has only 16 informative quartets and that its ancestral/derived orientation evidence is weaker. LG07 showed no corresponding shift (ΔD=-0.006, p=0.556), providing a contrasting inversion region in the same dataset.

Overall, local genealogies within LG01 and LG02, and supportively within LG12, are unusually aligned with the arrangement partition relative to other positions on the same linkage group. These analyses do not establish causality, do not fit MSRC parameters, and do not make Atlantic cod a direct multi-species persistence-through-speciation test.

As a post-freeze sensitivity analysis, we also asked whether treating many adjacent inversion windows as separate input trees changes quartet-summary population-tree inference. Local quartet-support profiles recapitulated the window-level pattern: inside-inversion support was arrangement-dominant for LG01, LG02, and LG12, whereas LG07 was mixed. The corresponding mean inside q_arrangement values were 1.000 for LG01, 0.946 for LG02, 0.343 for LG07, and 0.965 for LG12.

ASTRAL4 analyses of the published 250-kb population-tree windows did not recover the frozen collinear baseline topology under any primary treatment. The all-window tree recovered 1 of 9 baseline internal splits (unrooted RF=16), the fully outside-window tree recovered 3 of 9 baseline splits (RF=12), and the fully inside-window tree recovered 2 of 9 baseline splits (RF=14). Thus, excluding inversion intervals moved the summary tree modestly toward the collinear baseline but did not restore it.

Downweighting linked inversion windows produced the same qualitative result. When all 245 fully outside windows were retained and each inversion contributed at most one inside window per replicate, none of 100 deterministic ASTRAL4 replicates exactly matched the frozen baseline; the median normalized RF distance was 0.667, and the mean fraction of recovered baseline splits was 0.288. These ASTRAL4 analyses are therefore best interpreted as a linked-window sensitivity check rather than evidence that ASTRAL4 is wrong or that the Atlantic cod system by itself demonstrates MSRC.
