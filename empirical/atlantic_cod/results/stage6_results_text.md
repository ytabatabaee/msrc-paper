Local quartet-support profiles recapitulated the main Atlantic cod spatial result. Inside-inversion support was arrangement-dominant for LG01, LG02, and LG12, with mean q_arrangement values of 1.000, 0.946, and 0.965, respectively. LG07 was mixed inside the inversion interval, with mean q_arrangement=0.343, q_baseline=0.296, and q_third=0.361.

ASTRAL4 population summary-tree inference on the published 250-kb local population-tree windows did not recover the frozen 12-population collinear baseline topology. The all-window treatment used 426 input trees and recovered 1 of 9 baseline internal splits (unrooted RF=16, normalized RF=0.889). The fully outside-window treatment used 245 windows outside all frozen inversion intervals and recovered 3 of 9 baseline splits (RF=12, normalized RF=0.667). The fully inside-window treatment used 176 inversion windows and recovered 2 of 9 baseline splits (RF=14, normalized RF=0.778).

Leave-one-inversion-out treatments showed that removing LG01 inside windows moved the inferred tree closest to the outside-only result, recovering 3 of 9 baseline splits with normalized RF=0.667. Removing LG02 or LG07 inside windows recovered 2 of 9 baseline splits, and removing LG12 inside windows recovered 1 of 9, matching the all-window baseline-split recovery.

Progressive downweighting of linked inversion windows did not restore the frozen collinear baseline. When all 245 outside windows were retained and each inversion contributed at most one sampled inside window per replicate, none of 100 deterministic ASTRAL4 replicates exactly matched the baseline. The m=1 treatment had median normalized RF=0.667, range 0.667-0.778, and mean baseline-split recovery 0.288. Across larger finite m values, mean baseline-split recovery ranged from 0.168 to 0.301. These results indicate that linked inversion windows can affect quartet-summary population-tree inference, but they do not show that ASTRAL4 is incorrect, prove MSRC, or make Atlantic cod a direct multi-species persistence-through-speciation test.
