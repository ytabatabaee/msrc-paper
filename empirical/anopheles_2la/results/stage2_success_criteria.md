# Stage 2 Success Criteria

These criteria are fixed before real genealogy analysis.

## Strong Support For Arrangement-Associated Genealogy

1. At least one prospectively frozen eligible design is nonempty.
2. The arrangement-predicted topology is enriched inside 2La relative to flanks.
3. Direction agrees across independent design units.
4. The effect survives block-aware analysis.
5. The topology signal localizes spatially to the inversion region.

## Especially Strong Evidence

Arrangement-replacement contrasts show topology changing with structural
background rather than simply species label.

## Negative Result

No specific enrichment of the predeclared arrangement topology inside 2La.

## Inconclusive

Too few eligible frozen quartets, too little callable sequence, or too many
unresolved trees.

If Stage 1A completes scientifically but Design A, Design B, and Design C are
all empty, Stage 1B stops with `NO_ELIGIBLE_PROSPECTIVE_DESIGNS`. Stage 1B
eligibility is design-agnostic. A scientifically complete Stage 1A may enable
Design A, Design B, Design C, any combination, or no analysis if no
prospectively eligible designs exist. Zero Design-A quartets is not itself a
reason to alter the analysis plan. The all-empty outcome is inconclusive /
underpowered, not evidence against MSRC.

Do not redefine these criteria after seeing real results.
