# Quartet/recombination-track caption

Direct local quartet support across the published chr17 5-kb windows is shown
for all available tips, with lightly plotted raw windows and fixed 250-kb
means. The lower tracks show the source-defined phylogeny-based
recombination-state classification for domesticus, musculus, and castaneus in
nonoverlapping 500-kb bins. These categories describe inferred placement of
pseudo-t haplotypes in local trees; they are not direct recombination-rate
measurements in cM/Mb. The aligned display is descriptive and does not treat
adjacent windows as independent observations.
