# Quartet/recombination-track caption

Direct local quartet support across the published chr17 5-kb windows is shown
for all available tips, with lightly plotted raw windows and fixed 250-kb
means. `q_species`, `q_t_alt`, and `q_other` identify biological quartet
topologies and are not globally equivalent to ASTRAL q1/q2/q3. The lower
tracks directly use the authors' archived per-window topology classifications,
mapped to their source-defined phylogeny-based recombination-state categories
for domesticus, musculus, and castaneus in nonoverlapping 500-kb bins. The
y-axis is the fraction of 5-kb windows in each 500-kb bin assigned to each
source-defined class. This is not a direct recombination-rate estimate in
cM/Mb. The aligned display is descriptive and does not treat adjacent windows
as independent observations.
