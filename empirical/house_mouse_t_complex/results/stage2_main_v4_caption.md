# Manuscript main v4 figure caption

The House-mouse t-complex shows arrangement-state-conditioned genealogy and
mixed-state quartet distortion rather than a single long alternative-topology
block. (A) Two four-group ASTRAL cladograms: standard/noncarrier tips recover
`Q_SPECIES`, with CU = 0.056283 and localPP = 0.999985, while all standard plus
reconstructed pseudo-t haplotypes recover `Q_T_ALT`, with CU = 0.049097 and
localPP = 1.000000. The corresponding named quartet frequencies are
q_species/q_t_alt/q_other = 0.369910/0.343656/0.286434 for standard-only and
0.323323/0.365364/0.311313 for all tips. ASTRAL inference is unrooted;
*M. spretus* is used only to orient the display. Named q values refer to
biological topologies and are not fixed ASTRAL q1/q2/q3 labels. (B) Exact
quartet composition for all eight domesticus/musculus/castaneus arrangement
patterns, with S denoting standard and T denoting pseudo-t. Homogeneous SSS
and TTT favor Q_SPECIES; STS and TTS favor Q_T_ALT; TST favors Q_OTHER. (C)
Pattern weights and weighted contributions to q_species − q_t_alt. The
contributions sum to the all-tip quartet distribution and show how the six
mixed-state classes alter the aggregate; mixed-state quartets comprise 64.6%
of induced quartets, with STS and TTS providing the strongest negative
contributions. (D) Deterministic balanced resampling across 1,000 replicates.
Mixed-state sampling shifts the resampling distribution toward Q_T_ALT. B2 is
composition-balanced but has a different total sample size from B0 and B1.
The linked 5-kb windows are not independent biological loci.
