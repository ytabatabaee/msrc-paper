# Manuscript main v4 figure caption

The House-mouse t-complex shows arrangement-state-conditioned genealogy and
mixed-state quartet distortion rather than a single long alternative-topology
block. (A) Two four-group ASTRAL cladograms: standard/noncarrier tips recover
`Q_SPECIES`, while all standard plus reconstructed pseudo-t haplotypes recover
`Q_T_ALT`. ASTRAL inference is unrooted; *M. spretus* is used only to orient
the display. Named q values refer to biological topologies and are not fixed
ASTRAL q1/q2/q3 labels. (B) Exact quartet composition for all eight
domesticus/musculus/castaneus arrangement patterns, with S denoting standard
and T denoting pseudo-t. Homogeneous SSS and TTT favor Q_SPECIES; STS and TTS
favor Q_T_ALT; TST favors Q_OTHER. (C) Pattern weights and weighted
contributions to q_species − q_t_alt. The contributions sum to the all-tip
quartet distribution and show how mixed classes alter the aggregate. (D)
Deterministic balanced resampling across 1,000 replicates. B2 is
composition-balanced but has a different total sample size from B0 and B1.
The linked 5-kb windows are not independent biological loci.
