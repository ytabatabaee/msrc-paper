Conditional arrangement-state quartet experiment. (A) The species topology is
`12|34` (`Q_SPECIES`), while a recombination-suppressed genomic interval is
conditioned on arrangement configuration `1010`: taxa 1 and 3 carry state 1,
and taxa 2 and 4 carry state 0. The schematic does not represent a forward
rearrangement origin. (B) Exact quartet probabilities are shown as solid
curves with Monte Carlo estimates overlaid as points for four representative
structured-interval durations. (C) The same exact probability vectors trace
trajectories in the quartet simplex; longer intervals and lower switching
move the distribution toward the arrangement-associated `13|24` (`Q_ALT`)
corner. (D) The full tested grid shows exact `q_ALT-q_SPECIES`, with the
strongest bias at `t=2, m=0`. Same-background lineages coalesce at
`lambda0=lambda1=1`, switching is symmetric at `m01=m10=m`, and unresolved
quartets are resolved symmetrically among the three topologies.

This is a conditional structured-interval experiment and does not yet
simulate the forward origin and persistence of the rearrangement through a
species tree or establish full MSRC species-tree inconsistency.
