Conditional arrangement-state quartet experiment. Four lineages enter a
structured interval with arrangement configuration `1010`, so lineages 1 and
3 occupy one arrangement background and lineages 2 and 4 the other. Lineages
switch backgrounds symmetrically at rate `m`, while same-background lineages
coalesce at `lambda0=lambda1=1`. The arrangement-associated quartet is
therefore `13|24` (`Q_ALT`), whereas the species topology is defined as
`12|34` (`Q_SPECIES`). The heatmap shows the exact conditional difference
`q_ALT-q_SPECIES` as a function of structured-interval duration and switching
rate. Lower switching and longer structured intervals produce the strongest
arrangement-associated quartet bias. If a quartet remains unresolved after
the interval, the current conditional simulator resolves it symmetrically
among the three quartet topologies.

This is a conditional structured-interval experiment and does not yet
simulate the forward origin and persistence of the rearrangement through a
species tree.
