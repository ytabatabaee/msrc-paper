# Next step after Experiment 2B

Experiment 2B found no unconditional `Δ>0` cell, but rare terminal 2:2
histories retained strong conditional alternative support. The next run should
test whether that conditional signal can survive more complete history
averaging.

Recommended refinement:

- retain the balanced four-taxon tree and unconditional sampling;
- focus on `Ne ∈ {20,100}`, `τ ∈ {0.1,0.5}`, origin depths `.25/.75`, initial
  frequencies `.2/.5`, and cross fractions `.1/1.0`;
- prioritize cells with high daughter-branch persistence and frequent 2:2
  patterns, while retaining matched ordinary MSC controls;
- use at least 500 independent histories per selected cell and 20 loci/history;
- pre-register the candidate cells and use simultaneous confidence intervals
  across the selected search set;
- report `1010` and `0101` separately and jointly, without conditioning the
  primary statistic on either pattern.

Do not expand to larger species trees or ASTRAL until this targeted history
level is complete. If the expanded run remains negative, the evidence will
support the interpretation that conditional arrangement-associated quartet bias
is diluted by loss, fixation, and the low frequency of the relevant terminal
patterns under this neutral mechanistic design.
